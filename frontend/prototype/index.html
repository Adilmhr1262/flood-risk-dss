      Safety Guidelines

      What to do before, during and after a flood

      🎒
      Before a flood: Prepare an emergency kit with water, food, medicines, documents and a torch. Know your nearest shelter and evacuation route.
      📻
      Stay informed: Follow official alerts from PDMA Sindh and NDMA. Do not rely on rumors on social media.
      🚶
      During a flood: Move to higher ground immediately. Never walk or drive through flowing water deeper than 15 cm.
      ⚡
      Electrical safety: Turn off electricity and gas before leaving home. Avoid touching electrical equipment if wet.
      💧
      After a flood: Drink only boiled or treated water. Do not return home until authorities declare it safe.
